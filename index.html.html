How Ritual Network Works

⚡ What is Ritual Network and what does this platform do?+
Ritual Network is the social and reputation layer built on top of the Ritual blockchain ecosystem. This platform lets builders create an onchain identity, endorse each other, seal time-locked messages, and send token gifts — all recorded on the Ritual Testnet. It's your permanent builder profile and legacy in the Ritual ecosystem.
🦊 How do I connect my wallet and get started?+
Click Connect Wallet in the top right. You can connect via MetaMask (recommended — it auto-configures Ritual Testnet for you), or enter your wallet address manually. Then link your X (Twitter) handle to pull in your public profile and create your builder identity on Ritual Network. Once connected, your profile goes live on the Builder Directory and Leaderboard.
🔒 What are Legacy Drops and how do I seal one?+
Legacy Drops are time-locked messages that are sealed now and revealed on a future date you choose. Think of them as onchain time capsules — for the community, a specific builder, or your future self.

To seal one: go to Seal → Write Drop, optionally fetch a recipient's X profile, write your title and message, set an unlock date, choose visibility (Public / Community / Recipient only) and drop type (Message, Promise, Dedication, Future Self), then hit Seal This Drop. Your drop appears on the Legacy Drops Wall and generates a shareable certificate.
✅ What is a Vouch and how does it work?+
A Vouch is a wallet-signed permanent endorsement of another builder. It goes on their public profile and counts toward their reputation score on the Leaderboard.

To vouch: go to Seal → Vouch Builder, fetch the builder's X profile, write a specific endorsement (up to 200 chars), select your role (Builder, Shipper, Community Helper, or Collaborator), and hit Sign & Vouch Permanently.

You can also Quick Vouch directly from a builder's card on the Builder Directory. Optionally, paste their wallet address to send them Ritual Testnet tokens as a bonus recognition gift alongside the vouch.
🎁 How do I gift Ritual Testnet tokens to another builder?+
Go to Seal → Gift Tokens. You can optionally fetch the recipient's X profile. Then paste their wallet address to send them real Ritual Testnet ETH — this triggers an actual onchain transaction from your wallet.

  • If a wallet address is provided → a real ETH transfer is sent on Ritual Testnet.
  • If no wallet address → a symbolic onchain note is recorded instead.
Set the amount (in ETH), pick an occasion, add a message, and hit Send Ritual Testnet Tokens. You'll get a confirmation with the transaction hash and a shareable certificate.

Note: You need Ritual Testnet ETH in your own wallet to send tokens. Make sure you're connected to Ritual Testnet (Chain ID: 1752274).
🏆 What is the Leaderboard and how is it ranked?+
The Leaderboard ranks builders across two dimensions: Top Vouched (most endorsements received) and Most Apps Shipped. Rankings update live as the community vouches and builders ship apps. Your position on the leaderboard is also shown on your My Profile page. Vouching for builders you believe in is the primary way to grow community reputation.
👤 What's on My Profile and how do I customize it?+
Your profile shows your name, X handle, wallet address, bio, tags, and stats (Vouches, Drops, Apps, Rank). After connecting your wallet and linking your X account, your profile is live. You can click ✏️ Edit Bio to update your bio at any time — changes save instantly. Your profile also shows all your sealed Legacy Drops in the "My Legacy Drops" tab.
📜 What are the Certificates and can I share them?+
After every action (sealing a drop, vouching, or gifting tokens), Ritual Network generates a holographic onchain certificate — a downloadable image showing the sender, recipient, action details, and a unique certificate ID. You can View Certificate, Download it, or share it directly to X (Twitter) with a pre-filled post that tags the recipient and includes your Ritual Network link.
🌐 What network does this run on? Do I need real ETH?+
Everything runs on Ritual Testnet — a test blockchain, so no real money is involved. Chain ID: 1752274 · RPC: ritual-testnet-rpc.eu-north-2.gateway.fm

MetaMask adds this network automatically when you connect. To send testnet tokens you'll need Ritual Testnet ETH in your wallet — check the Ritual community for testnet faucet access.
